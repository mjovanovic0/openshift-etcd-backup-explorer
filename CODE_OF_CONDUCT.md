# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes contributions from everyone. Participants are expected to
treat each other with respect, whatever their background or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that reviewers and maintainers may disagree with you, and that a
  maintainer may decline a change without it being a judgement on you.
- Assume good faith. Most friction in text is a misreading, not an attack.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/mjovanovic0). Reports will be handled discreetly.
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that breach
this document.
